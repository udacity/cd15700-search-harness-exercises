Romania VAT guide

Romania raised its standard VAT rate from 19% to 21% on 1 August 2025.

Update, 3 November 2026

Romania has reversed the August 2025 increase. Under Law 214/2026, the standard VAT rate is 19% for invoices issued from 1 November 2026. The 21% rate above now applies only to invoices issued before that date, and invoices issued since then at 21% have to be corrected.
